01 // Research Objective & Scope

Parkinson's Disease is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder characterized by the gradual loss of dopaminergic neurons. While motor symptoms like tremors and rigidity are commonly used for clinical diagnosis, vocal impairment (dysphonia) is often one of the earliest indicators, appearing years before gross motor dysfunction.

NeuroInsight AI is an applied machine learning research project exploring the non-invasive prediction of Parkinson's Disease using vocal acoustic biomarkers. By utilizing a custom-engineered Feature Performance Index (fPI) alongside advanced tree-based classifiers like XGBoost, this project demonstrates how combination features and structured tabular classification can detect early-stage dysphonia with high precision and interpretability.

Note: This repository represents a research-oriented predictive modeling study using voice recording datasets. It is not designed, certified, or intended for clinical medical diagnosis or diagnostic product use.

02 // Feature Performance Index (fPI) Formulation

The raw dataset consists of vocal acoustic measurements from 198 subjects (140 Parkinson's, 58 healthy controls). Features cover pitch frequency, amplitude perturbation (Jitter/Shimmer), noise measures, and fractal dynamics.

To improve mathematical separation between cohorts, I engineered a domain-specific composite metric called the Feature Performance Index (fPI), combining fractal scaling, chaotic complexity, and variation:

$$\text{fPI} = \log_{10}\Big( \text{DFA} \cdot \text{D2} \cdot \text{spread2} \Big)$$

DFA (Fractal Scaling)

Detrended Fluctuation Analysis measures the self-similarity and long-range fractal scaling exponents of the voice signal.

D2 (Chaotic Complexity)

Correlation dimension estimates the signal's complexity and characterizes vocal fold oscillation dynamics.

spread2 (Pitch Variation)

Nonlinear measure quantifying fundamental frequency variations to isolate micro-tremors in vocal control.

03 // Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

ADR-005 // Non-Linear Vocal Acoustic Feature Synthesis Status: Accepted

Context: Raw vocal perturbation attributes (Jitter, Shimmer) suffer from high multicollinearity and non-Gaussian distributions, degrading linear SVM classifier performance.

Decision: Synthesize a composite Feature Performance Index $\text{fPI} = \log_{10}(\text{DFA} \times \text{D2} \times \text{spread2})$ that projects fractal self-similarity, chaotic dimension, and pitch variance into a unified logarithmic domain.

Consequences: Yields superior decision boundaries in gradient-boosted trees, achieving 96.6% accuracy and an AUC score of 0.958.
